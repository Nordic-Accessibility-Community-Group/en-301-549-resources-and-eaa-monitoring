# EAA enforcement tracking

> Note that the tools for reporting to the monitoring agencies in different countries could be just for one specific area (such as E-books). If you do find incorrect or missing information please add a PR, message Erik Gustafsson Spagnoli through [Slack](http://web-a11y.slack.com/), [LinkedIn](http://a11y.se/) or send an email to [erik@axesslab.com](mailto:erik@axesslab.com).

        <li>In an interim order dated 4 June 2026, the Tribunal judiciaire de Caen ordered Carrefour France to provide e-commerce services complying with French accessibility requirements. A €500 daily penalty applies if non-compliance continues six months after the order.</li>
        <li>The court also ordered Carrefour to pay a total of €10,000 in provisional damages to apiDV and Droit Pluriel, the costs of the proceedings, and €3,000 under article 700 of the French Code of Civil Procedure.</li>

The ruling is notable because the court relied on the older French €250 million turnover threshold for online public communication services. apiDV, Droit Pluriel and Intérêt à Agir argue that this misreads the separate EAA transposition in the French Consumer Code, and have appealed to the Cour d'appel de Douai.</li>
